Our Journey

From startup to industry leader.

Founded in 2015 with a vision to revolutionize healthcare technology solutions, and serving 500+ clients today.

  2. 2015

    Foundation

    UltraCorr IT was founded with a vision to revolutionize healthcare technology solutions, starting with medical coding services for small practices.

  3. 2017

    Expansion

    Expanded services to include comprehensive IT infrastructure development and achieved AHIMA certification for our coding team.

  4. 2019

    Innovation

    Launched our proprietary healthcare analytics platform and established partnerships with major EHR vendors including Epic and Cerner.

  5. 2021

    Growth

    Reached 300+ healthcare clients nationwide and achieved SOC 2 Type II compliance, establishing ourselves as a trusted healthcare IT partner.

  6. 2023 - Now

    Present

    Leading the industry with AI-powered coding solutions, serving 500+ clients, and expanding into telehealth infrastructure and cloud migration services.
